# Commercial licensing

Tachyon is free and open source under **GPL-3.0-or-later** ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). For most
people that's all you need — **install it, run it, even modify it for your own use, at no
cost and with no obligation.**

A **commercial license** exists for the cases where GPL-3.0's copyleft is a blocker:

- **Embedding** Tachyon (or a derivative) in a **closed-source** product you distribute.
- **Distributing a modified version** without releasing your changes under the GPL.
- Shipping it inside an offering where the **source-disclosure** obligation isn't acceptable.

The commercial license grants those rights — use and distribute Tachyon without the GPL's
copyleft obligations — under terms agreed per case.

**You do NOT need a commercial license to:** install Tachyon from the Marketplace, use it at
work, run it on private/internal projects, or modify it for your own internal use. Plain use
never triggers GPL obligations — those apply only when you *distribute* a derivative.
